About Kalash Valley

The Kalash Valley is located in the Chitral district of Khyber Pakhtunkhwa, nestled in the Hindu Kush mountains. It is home to the Kalash people — an ancient, non-Muslim minority community with their own distinct language, customs, and polytheistic religion dating back thousands of years.

The Kalash are known for their colorful festivals, traditional wooden temples (JastKhans), unique embroidered clothing, and warm hospitality. The three Kalash valleys — Bumburet, Rumbur, and Birir — each offer their own charm and cultural experiences.

Kalash Festivals

  • Chilam Joshi (May) — Spring festival welcoming the new season
  • Uchaw (August) — Summer harvest festival
  • Chaumos (December) — The grand winter festival with dancing and singing

Things to Do

  • Visit traditional Kalash villages and wooden temples
  • Experience local crafts, music, and traditional dance
  • Trek through the scenic Hindu Kush valleys
  • Visit Chitral Fort and Shandur Pass
  • Explore Tirich Mir viewpoints — the highest peak of Hindu Kush
