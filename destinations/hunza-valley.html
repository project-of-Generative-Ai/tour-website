About Hunza Valley

Hunza Valley is a popular tourist destination in Pakistan known for its stunning natural beauty and rich cultural heritage. The valley is located in the Gilgit-Baltistan territory and is surrounded by snow-capped mountains, lush green valleys, glaciers, and crystal-clear streams. The most popular route to reach Hunza is via the Karakoram Highway (KKH), which connects Pakistan to China and passes through the valley.

The Hunza people have a distinct culture shaped by their history, geography, and religion. The valley is home to several historical sites, including the Altit and Baltit forts, which provide insight into the region's rich cultural heritage spanning over 2,000 years.

Top Attractions

  • Attabad Lake — A stunning turquoise lake formed by a 2010 landslide
  • Baltit Fort — 700-year-old fort overlooking the valley
  • Altit Fort — One of Pakistan's oldest forts
  • Khunjerab Pass — World's highest paved international border crossing
  • Rush Lake — One of the highest lakes in the world, for trekkers
  • Borith Lake — Serene lake near Gulmit village

Adventure Activities

Hunza Valley offers trekking, mountaineering, rock climbing, and camping. The region is home to challenging peaks including Rakaposhi (7,788 m), Ultar Sar, and Diran Peak, attracting climbers from around the world.
