About Astore Valley

Astore is a 120-kilometer-long, 5,000-square-kilometer valley in the Gilgit-Baltistan state in the north of Pakistan. The valley sits at the eastern base of Nanga Parbat — the world's 9th highest mountain at 8,125 m — often called the "Killer Mountain" due to its dangerous ascent.

The valley's landscape is breathtakingly beautiful and features remarkable geographical variation, giving the adventurous traveler endless trekking options and the chance to immerse themselves in a culture that few tourists have experienced. Astore Valley stretches from the confluence of the Indus and Astore rivers all the way up to the stunning Deosai Plains.

Why Visit Astore Valley?

Often referred to as the "Paradise of Northern Pakistan," Astore Valley is renowned for its enchanting vistas and tranquil ambiance. Weather

Summer (Peak Season)

Summer is the peak tourist season with mild and pleasant weather. Daytime temperatures range between 20°C and 30°C (68°F–86°F), while nights drop to around 10°C–15°C. Occasional rainfall can occur, so carry a raincoat.

Winter

Winter is cold and harsh with heavy snowfall at higher elevations. Temperatures can drop well below freezing (−5°C to 10°C during the day). Heavy woolen clothing, thick jackets, hats, gloves, and warm footwear are essential.

Must-Visit Places

  • Rama Lake — a stunning high-altitude lake with a beautiful meadow
  • Deosai National Park — one of the world's highest plateaus
  • Nanga Parbat Viewpoints
  • Rama Meadows — perfect for camping and hiking
  • Astore River — crystal-clear glacial river ideal for fishing
