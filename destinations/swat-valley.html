About Swat Valley

Swat Valley is renowned for its picturesque landscapes, snow-capped peaks, and glistening rivers. Often called the "Switzerland of the East," it is a paradise for nature lovers, adventure enthusiasts, and history buffs. The region has a fascinating history dating back to the ancient Gandhara civilization.

Top Attractions

  • Mahodand Lake — crystal-clear turquoise lake surrounded by mountains
  • Kalam Valley — lush forests, Ushu River and charming village
  • Malam Jabba — Pakistan's premier ski resort
  • Butkara Stupa — ancient Buddhist ruins from the Gandhara era
  • Swat Museum — remarkable collection of Gandhara art
