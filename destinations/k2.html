About K2

K2 — "The Savage Mountain" — is the world's second-highest peak at 8,611 m, on the Pakistan-China border in the Karakoram Range. The K2 Base Camp trek passes through the Baltoro Glacier — one of the longest glaciers outside the polar regions — with views of four 8,000 m peaks: K2, Broad Peak, Gasherbrum I and II.

Trek Highlights

  • Baltoro Glacier — 63 km long glacial highway
  • Concordia — the "Throne Room of the Mountain Gods"
  • Views of Broad Peak, Gasherbrum I & II
  • Urdukas Campsite — stunning views and green plateau
  • K2 Base Camp at the foot of the savage mountain

Requirements

Good physical fitness, prior trekking experience, and proper acclimatization required. A permit from PTDC is needed — our team handles all logistics, permits, and guides.
