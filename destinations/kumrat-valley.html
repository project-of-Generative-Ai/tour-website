About Kumrat Valley

Kumrat Valley is located in Upper Dir, Khyber Pakhtunkhwa — a breathtaking valley blessed with dense pine and deodar forests, the roaring Panjkora River, lush green meadows, and a vibrant local culture. It remains relatively off the tourist radar, making it perfect for those seeking untouched natural beauty.

The valley is famous for its wooden mosques, friendly locals, and some of the most pristine natural scenery in Pakistan. The combination of forests, river, and mountain views makes it a must-visit for nature lovers and adventure seekers alike.

Top Attractions

  • Jahaz Banda — a stunning high-altitude meadow resembling an aircraft runway
  • Panjkora River — ideal for camping along its banks
  • Do Kala Lake — serene alpine lake surrounded by forests
  • Traditional wooden mosques of Dir Kohistan
  • Katora Lake — a bowl-shaped hidden alpine lake

Best Time to Visit

May to October is ideal. July and August are peak season with lush greenery. The road to Kumrat can be difficult in winter due to heavy snowfall.
