About Fairy Meadows

Fairy Meadows is located in the northern region of Pakistan, situated at the base of Nanga Parbat — the ninth highest mountain in the world. The meadows, with their lush green carpets, wildflowers, and grazing sheep, create a serene and picturesque landscape. The area is often referred to as "Heaven on Earth" due to its stunning vistas and tranquil ambiance.

The journey to Fairy Meadows itself is an adventure, involving a jeep ride through winding mountain roads and a trek through dense pine forests. The reward, however, is worth it — a paradise-like setting that feels like stepping into a storybook.

Things to Do

  • Trek to Nanga Parbat Base Camp (Bayal Camp)
  • Camping under a sky full of stars
  • Hiking through alpine meadows and pine forests
  • Wildlife watching — Himalayan ibex, markhor
  • Visit the nearby Tattu village and experience local culture

Weather

Summer (May – September)

Pleasant temperatures from 15°C to 25°C during the day. Nights are cool (10°C–15°C). The best time for trekking and outdoor activities.

Winter (October – April)

Cold and harsh. Daytime temperatures from −5°C to 5°C. Nights can drop below −10°C with heavy snowfall. Not recommended for general visitors.
